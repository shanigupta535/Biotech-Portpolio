// Research & Lab Work

Hands-On Science

Experiments I've performed, techniques I've learned, and what each one taught me.

🧪 PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)

Amplification of DNA

Performed thermal cycling to amplify a target DNA segment through denaturation, annealing and extension stages.

💡 Learned: primer design basics, cycle optimization & contamination control.

⚡ Gel Electrophoresis

Separation of DNA fragments

Separated DNA fragments by size on an agarose gel and visualized bands under UV transillumination.

💡 Learned: agarose concentration, loading & band interpretation.

🧬 DNA Isolation

Extraction of genomic DNA

Isolated genomic DNA using cell lysis, protein precipitation and alcohol precipitation methods.

💡 Learned: lysis buffers, centrifugation & purity (A260/A280) checks.

✂️ Restriction Digestion

Enzyme-based DNA cleavage

Used restriction endonucleases to cut DNA at specific recognition sites for downstream analysis.

💡 Learned: palindromic sequences & sticky vs blunt ends.

🦠 Bacterial Culture

Microbial growth & streaking

Cultured bacterial strains on agar plates and broth media using aseptic technique and streaking methods.

💡 Learned: sterilization, colony isolation & growth curves.

🔍 Sequence Analysis (In Silico)

BLAST & database mining

Retrieved and aligned sequences from NCBI, performed BLAST searches and interpreted alignment scores.

💡 Learned: E-values, identity scores & phylogenetic basics.
